User data deletion instructions

You can request deletion of information The Food Advisor holds about you, including information associated with a connected Facebook Page. You do not need to send us a Facebook password, access token, or other credential.

How to request deletion

  1. Email stuart@thefoodadvisor.co.uk with the subject “The Food Advisor data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us which The Food Advisor account or connected Facebook Page your request concerns, and which information you want deleted. Send the request from the email address associated with your account if you can. Do not include passwords or access tokens.
  3. We will verify that you are authorised to make the request, review the information we hold, and respond to you about the deletion and any information we must retain for legal reasons.

Disconnect a social account

An authorised admin can disconnect a Facebook Page from Admin → Automation → Social → Connected Accounts. This removes the locally saved publishing credential and stops future scheduled posts for that connection, but does not automatically erase historical posts or records. Include those records in your email request if you want them reviewed for deletion. You can also remove The Food Advisor from your Facebook account’s Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites to revoke access at Meta.

For more about how information is handled, read our Privacy Policy. If you have trouble making a request, use the email address above.
