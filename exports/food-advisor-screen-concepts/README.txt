THE FOOD ADVISOR — SCREEN CONCEPTS

16 proposed mobile screen designs, exported at 1170 × 2532 pixels.
These are design mockups, not screenshots of implemented app features.
Restaurant data, maps, metrics and content are illustrative. AI services and £99/month payments are proposed, not activated.
Longer screens can be scrolled in the canvas previews; these PNGs show the opening viewport of each screen.
The live app has not been changed by this design work.
