Paid restaurant searches may continue for directory discovery, but a returned listing is not an outreach-ready contact. Check its known public website for a qualifying business mailbox; if none is found or the website cannot be checked, retain the restaurant in a separate email-research list rather than treating it as ready to mail. Do not mix this list with unverified OSM candidates, which have different source-rights and claim rules.

**Why:** The user considers a paid search result without a usable email poor value for outreach, but explicitly chose website research after discovery rather than blocking all paid restaurant searches. Google Places supplies restaurant details and a website, not email. Existing website rechecks should not repeat a paid Places lookup.

**How to apply:** For each newly discovered paid listing, keep the paid request and website extraction as separate steps; label contact qualification honestly and preserve failed/no-email records for further research. Do not claim syntax-checked mailboxes are deliverability-verified, or automatically retry historical failed sends merely because an email was found.
