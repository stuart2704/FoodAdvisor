---
name: Candidate publication gates
description: User decisions on OSM candidate review, automated scoring, and future publication.
---

New OSM candidates remain unpublished for now. A verified owner claim linked to the restaurant and recorded source-rights evidence are both required before a future candidate-to-restaurant publication path is enabled. A high completeness/confidence score may prioritize internal review, but must not prove ownership, rights, or publication eligibility.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected owner-claim and source-rights gates, then chose to keep new candidates unpublished rather than create hidden draft restaurants. The current candidate records have no owner-claim linkage or source-rights proof.

**How to apply:** Keep candidate review and rejection separate from public restaurant verification. Do not treat a high-score candidate or an admin candidate-review action as a published listing. If building publication later, first design the claim linkage, rights evidence, attribution, and safe promotion transaction.
