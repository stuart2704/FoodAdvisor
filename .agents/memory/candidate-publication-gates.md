---
name: Candidate publication gates
description: User decisions on OSM candidate review, contact proof, source rights, and publication.
---

New OSM candidates remain unpublished until a reviewed invitation, verified contact, and separate admin approvals of ownership and source-rights evidence precede activation. A high completeness/confidence score may prioritize internal review but must not prove ownership, rights, or publication eligibility. Email-code verification proves inbox access only, not business ownership. Do not turn on automatic invitations without a trusted, approved business-contact source. Admin review of a documented independent business contact source is an acceptable approval path; OSM tags and imported scores alone are not.

**Why:** The user explicitly required owner-claim and source-rights gates before publication. OpenStreetMap imports do not inherently identify a person authorized to claim a business, and a candidate score does not establish contact authority or reuse rights.

**How to apply:** Keep candidate review and rejection separate from public restaurant verification. Keep drafts private, preserve source attribution, and make publication contingent on current approvals for the listing being published. Treat ambiguous invitation delivery as requiring review, not automatic retry.
